The hotel

One address for a good night's sleep and a proper meal.

Hotel Shera and Restaurant is a modern, purpose-built property: clean air-conditioned rooms upstairs, a full family restaurant on the ground floor, and a rooftop garden that turns into the best table in town after sunset.

We are built for the way people actually travel here — arriving late, leaving early, bringing family, needing somewhere safe to park. The desk is staffed around the clock, the kitchen runs well past dinner, and nothing about the stay is complicated.

  • Air-conditioned rooms with hot water and fresh linen
  • Multi-cuisine family restaurant, veg & non-veg
  • Open-air rooftop garden dining
  • Hall for birthdays, receptions and corporate meets
  • Ample off-street parking and power backup
